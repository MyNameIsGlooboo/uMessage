## Fonts

Bundled under `app/src/main/res/font` as static weight instances:

- **Google Sans Flex**, copyright 2015 Google LLC, the Google Sans Flex Project Authors, SIL Open Font License 1.1.
- **Roboto**, copyright 2011 the Roboto Project Authors / Google LLC, Apache License 2.0.
- **Vazirmatn** (Regular, Medium, Bold; v33.003 from https://github.com/rastikerdar/vazirmatn), copyright 2015 the Vazirmatn Project Authors, SIL Open Font License 1.1. Used as the fallback for Persian and Arabic text. Modified: the vertical metrics (hhea and OS/2 typo ascent and descent) are set to Roboto's so Persian lines are as tall as English ones; the glyphs are unchanged.

The full OFL 1.1 text is in `licenses/OFL-1.1.txt` (also at https://openfontlicense.org). The in-app notices are under Settings > About > Licenses.

## Sounds

The two in-chat sounds under `app/src/main/res/raw` (`chat_sent.wav`, `chat_received.wav`) are short tones synthesized for uMessage with a script (plain sine waves). They carry no third-party licence and are covered by the project's GPL-3.0.
